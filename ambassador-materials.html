Introduction to LAGNAF™ Network
In Progress

LAGNAF™ Network is a private, controlled-access business infrastructure system. It is not a public platform, a franchise, or a service subscription. It is a closed operator network built around digital asset ownership, brand infrastructure, and scalable systems for serious operators.

As a Brand Ambassador, your role is singular: introduce qualified individuals to the entry gateway. You do not sell, pitch, or explain pricing. You extend invitations. The system handles everything from entry through onboarding.

  • All introductions are made through your personal referral link only
  • You never disclose pricing, system details, or operator terms
  • Your identity as a Brand Ambassador is confidential — not disclosed to prospects
  • All referrals are tracked automatically via your LAGNAF code

Your success as a Brand Ambassador depends entirely on the quality — not the quantity — of your introductions. One serious operator is worth more than ten unqualified inquiries.

The Network Standard
Locked

The LAGNAF™ Network operates under a strict standard of conduct for all participants — operators and Brand Ambassadors alike. Understanding and upholding this standard is non-negotiable.

Confidentiality. Everything disclosed within the system — pricing, operator identities, network structure, digital asset valuations — is governed by the NDA you executed at entry. This extends to your role as a Brand Ambassador. You may not discuss system specifics with anyone outside the network.

Brand Integrity. You represent the network by extension. Any conduct that damages the brand — misrepresentation, unauthorized marketing materials, public claims about income — is grounds for immediate removal and forfeiture of pending commissions.

Referral Quality. Qualified operators have verifiable business activity, a clear use case for the infrastructure, and the financial capacity to participate. Do not submit referrals who cannot meet these criteria. Low-quality referrals do not convert — and you are only compensated on closed transactions.

  • Never share system pricing publicly or in writing
  • Never create marketing materials without written approval
  • Never make income guarantees or forward projections to prospects
  • Always refer prospects to the entry gateway — never to a team member directly
3
The Referral Process
Locked

Your referral process is a one-step action: share your personal referral link with a qualified prospect. When they visit the link, your LAGNAF code is captured automatically — no manual tracking, no follow-up codes to enter.

How the system captures your referral:

  • Your link appends your code to the entry gateway URL: entry-gateway.html?ref=LAGNAF-XXXXXX
  • The system reads the code on page load and stores it in the prospect's session
  • Even if they navigate away and return, your attribution is preserved
  • When they complete entry, your code is written to their contact record in HubSpot automatically

What happens after they submit:

  • They proceed through NDA execution and UIN issuance — you are not involved
  • The system routes them to the appropriate operator or Brand Ambassador track
  • If they select and pay for a build, your commission is triggered and logged
  • You can track referral status in your Brand Ambassador dashboard

You do not need to follow up, schedule calls, or manage prospects through the funnel. Your job ends at the introduction. The system handles the rest.
