Earn Through Strategic Referrals

The LAGNAF™ Brand Ambassador Program is a structured referral framework designed for serious operators. Refer qualified operators to the network and earn commissions based on the transaction type.

Each referred client qualifies under one commission model — either a direct purchase or a lease-to-own arrangement. Compensation is determined by the transaction type completed.

  • 15% commission on eligible direct purchases paid in full
  • 3% commission on received & verified monthly lease payments
  • 1% lump sum on verified final lease payoff
  • 90-day onboarding window for permanent attribution
  • Commission paid on verified, received payments only
