Ambassador Commission Structure

Brand Ambassador Compensation

Two earning paths — direct purchase commissions and lease-to-own residuals.

Apply for Consideration

Model 1 — Direct Purchase

Outright Purchase Commission

15%

Earn 15% commission on eligible purchases paid in full. Referral must be registered within the 90-day validity window.

  • 15% on eligible purchases paid in full
  • Valid for 90 days from referral registration
  • Single lump-sum payout upon verified payment
  • Applies to all qualifying network products

Model 2 — Lease-to-Own ($50,000+)

Lease-to-Own Commission

3% + 1%

3% on received & verified monthly payments. 1% lump sum on verified final payoff. Includes free website design & setup (~3 weeks).

  • 3% on received & verified monthly payments
  • 1% of the verified final payoff — paid as lump sum
  • Applies to arrangements of $50,000 or more
  • Free website design and setup included

Commission Structure

Either — Or

Each referred client qualifies under one commission structure — either a direct purchase or a lease-to-own arrangement. Commissions are paid according to the applicable model.

Important Conditions

▸

90-Day Referral Window: All referrals are valid for 90 days from the date of registration. Transactions must be completed and verified within this window.

▸

Verified Payments Only: Commissions are paid exclusively on received and verified payments. Pending or disputed payments do not qualify until fully confirmed.

▸

Infrastructure Only: LAGNAF™ network LLC provides systems and infrastructure only. We do not operate or manage your business or your referrals' businesses.
