Brand Ambassador Program

A structured referral role within the LAGNAF™ network. Ambassadors operate through a verified .card identity — not a name. Activation requires full onboarding completion.

The Brand Ambassador Role

A LAGNAF™ Brand Ambassador is an introduction-based network expansion role. The function is singular: extend qualified invitations to the system. The ambassador does not pitch, does not negotiate, and does not manage clients through the funnel. The system handles everything from entry through onboarding.

Compensation is tied directly to verified operator transactions attributed to your referral code. This is not a sales role. It is a positioning role.

Foundational Infrastructure

.card System & Secure Activation Verification™ — Identity System

Every Brand Ambassador operates through a LAGNAF™ .card identity — not a personal name or a public profile. This is not an accessory. It is the operational foundation of your Brand Ambassador function.

.card identity

Your Brand Ambassador identity is assigned through the .card system. All referral activity is linked to this identity — not your personal information.

Secure Activation Verification™

Your activation is initialised during onboarding and linked to your .card identity through the Secure Activation Verification™ process. Server-side validation confirms deployment eligibility.

Attribution

When a prospect taps your card or follows your referral link, your code is captured automatically. There is no manual tracking. Attribution is system-handled.
