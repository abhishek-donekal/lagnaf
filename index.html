About the Network

Infrastructure, not a service.

LAGNAF™ network LLC is not a vendor. We are a closed operating network — accessible by invitation or through verified ambassador introduction. Our infrastructure gives operators a structured path to brand presence, system support, and connected growth.

Entry is not guaranteed. All access is gated behind identity verification and NDA execution. Pricing, asset details, and system infrastructure are disclosed only after completion.

LAGNAF™ network LLC provides systems and infrastructure only. We do not operate or manage your business.

How Access Works

Entry is structured. Not open.

01
Request Access
Submit your entry request through the gateway. Direct or via Brand Ambassador referral with a LAGNAF referral code. Your information is reviewed before any system detail is disclosed.
02
NDA Execution
A non-disclosure agreement is executed. Upon completion, a Unique Identification Number is issued. Your UIN is your identity within the system from this point forward.
03
Path Selection
Select your operating path: New Operator, Existing Operator, or Brand Ambassador. Each path routes to its corresponding system track and infrastructure.
04
System Deployment
Assets are deployed. Ambassador DOT identity is activated. Operator infrastructure is configured. You are operational within the network.
