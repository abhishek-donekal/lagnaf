What Your DOT Identity Means

Your business operates under this identity.

From this point forward, all referral activity, attribution, and compensation tracking runs under your DOT identity — not your personal name or contact information.

Referrals route through your master code.

When a prospect taps your DOT card or follows your referral link, your master code is captured automatically. Attribution is system-handled — there is no manual tracking required.

Compensation eligibility is tied to this code.

Commissions are only paid on transactions that carry your master code attribution. Referrals made without your code cannot be retroactively attributed.

The DOT card is your primary operational tool.

Your physical DOT NFC card is programmed to your referral link. Tapping the card opens the LAGNAF™ entry gateway with your code pre-loaded. This is how in-person introductions are made.
