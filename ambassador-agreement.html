Operating Terms

▸

Verified Payments Only. Commissions are paid exclusively on received and verified payments. Pending, disputed, or reversed payments do not qualify until fully confirmed.

▸

90-Day Referral Window. Referrals are valid for 90 days from registration. The referred operator must complete a verified payment within this window. Re-introduction resets the window.

▸

Secure Activation Verification™ Attribution Required. Commissions are only paid on transactions that carry verified activation attribution. Referrals made without verified attribution in the chain cannot be retroactively credited.

▸

Active Status Required. You must maintain active ambassador status — no violations, no conduct infractions — to receive commission disbursement. Removal from the network forfeits pending commissions.

▸

Tax Responsibility. You are solely responsible for any tax obligations arising from commissions received. LAGNAF™ network LLC does not withhold or file on your behalf.

▸

Confidentiality. All system details, pricing, operator identities, and network infrastructure disclosed following NDA execution are strictly confidential. This obligation extends to your role as an ambassador.
