The LAGNAF™ Brand Ambassador role is an introduction-based network expansion position. Ambassadors refer qualified operators to the system and earn commissions based on transaction type — 15% on direct purchases, 3% monthly on lease arrangements, and 1% lump sum on verified final payoff.
